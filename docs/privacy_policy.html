Privacy Policy for Ranuka Store

Last Updated: September 6, 2026 • Effective Date: September 6, 2026
Application: Ranuka Store (Thiruttani Quick) • Package: com.thiruttaniquick.customer
Overview: Ranuka Store ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile grocery delivery application Ranuka Store (Thiruttani Quick). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is collected, processed, and protected in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023).

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable grocery delivery services in Tiruttani and surrounding areas, we collect the following types of information:

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Contact & Identity Data: Mobile phone number (used for secure OTP login and delivery communication), full name, and email address.
  • Delivery Addresses: Street address, landmarks, city, state, and pin code (e.g., 631209, 631211) to deliver your orders accurately.
  • Communications: Customer support messages, ratings, reviews, and feedback.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Location Data (Precise & Approximate): We request access to your device's GPS location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to verify that your delivery address falls within our active delivery radius in Tiruttani and to help our delivery partners navigate to your doorstep. Location is collected only when the app is in use.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and app crash logs (via Firebase) to maintain app stability and optimize performance.
  • Order & Cart Activity: Products viewed, added to cart, search queries (including voice and phonetic search terms), and order history to personalize your shopping experience.

C. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not collect or store sensitive payment credentials such as credit card numbers, debit card CVVs, or net banking passwords. All online payments are handled directly by RBI-authorized payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay/UPI apps).
  • We do not track your background location when the app is closed.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Request Them

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required to automatically pinpoint your delivery address and ensure you are within our serviceable area in Tiruttani.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to send real-time order tracking updates ("Order Confirmed", "Out for Delivery", "Delivered") and important alerts. You can disable notifications anytime in your device settings.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) (Optional): Used exclusively when you tap the microphone button to perform voice search for groceries (e.g., "Atta", "Cooking Oil"). Audio is processed on-device or streamed transiently to Google's speech recognition engine; we never record or store ambient audio.
  • Internet & Network State: Required to communicate with our cloud servers and database.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

We work with trusted third-party providers who process data on our behalf in strict accordance with industry security standards:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, App Check / Play Integrity): For secure authentication, database management, push notifications, and preventing unauthorized app tampering. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to display occasional banner or sponsored ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and approximate location for ad serving and fraud detection. (Google Ads Policy)
  • Google Play Services: Required for device integrity verification, map rendering, and app distribution.

5. Advertising & Sponsored Content

Our app displays limited advertising through Google AdMob. Any promotional placements inside product sections are distinctly labeled with a "Sponsored" tag in full compliance with Google Play Ads Policy. You may reset or opt out of personalized advertising via your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures:

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access to production databases is strictly restricted to authorized administrative personnel.
  • Database access rules enforce row-level security so customers can only access their own user data and orders.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to comply with statutory accounting and tax regulations in India.

Right to Delete Your Data: You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app at:

Ranuka Store App > Profile Tab > Settings > Delete Account

Alternatively, you can email our Grievance Officer at support@ranukastore.in with your registered phone number. Upon receipt, your personal profile, saved addresses, and active sessions will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adult consumers and households capable of entering into binding purchasing contracts. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age. If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. When updates are published, the "Last Updated" date at the top will be refreshed, and noticeable notices will be displayed in the app for material changes.
