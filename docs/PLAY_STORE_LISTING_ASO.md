## 5. Google Play Policy Checklist

1. **Privacy Policy Link:**
   - Link: `https://ranuka-stores.neocities.org/` (or the hosted `privacy_policy.html`).
   - Must be pasted in: **Play Console &gt; App content &gt; Privacy Policy**.
2. **Ads Declaration:**
   - Go to **Play Console &gt; App content &gt; Ads**.
   - Select **"Yes, my app contains ads"** (since Google Mobile Ads / AdMob is included).
3. **App Access:**
   - Select **"All or some functionality in my app is restricted"**.
   - Provide test login credentials (test phone number + OTP or test email) so Google's review team can access the catalog and test ordering without requiring real Indian SMS OTP delivery.
4. **Target Audience & Content:**
   - Target age: **18 and over**.
   - Does your app appeal to children? &rarr; **No**.
5. **Government Apps:**
   - Is your app developed by or on behalf of a government? &rarr; **No**.
6. **Financial Features:**
   - Does your app provide financial features? &rarr; **No** (Grocery shopping app; payments are handled via external gateway).
